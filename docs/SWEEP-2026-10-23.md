# Real-repository sweep, 2026-10-23 (round r10; findings only, nothing fixed yet)

Harness: `bench/head-to-head/sweep/` (38 public repositories, shallow clones of depth 400 under `/private/tmp/pe-sweep`, pinned HEAD shas and ranges in `results.json`). 84 ranges, 1272 records, 2627 s on a shared machine. References are plain git or gitleaks 8.30.1 and were written in the harness, not taken from our code. Our CLI at `5a5e557`.

## Real bugs (to fix next, with tests)
1. `range --json` crash on pytest 5.3.3..5.3.4: `bin/range.mjs:254` reads `m.net.state` for a merge commit whose subject is `Revert "..."`; `net` stays null, exit 2.
2. `--json` output is cut at exactly 65536 bytes when stdout is a pipe (`bin/check.mjs:556` calls `process.exit` before the pipe drains). Affects `range`, `scope`, `stubs` on a large range (15 cases). A hook or agent reading the output through a pipe gets invalid JSON. Fix: set `process.exitCode` and let the stream drain.
3. Timeouts at 120 s for `range --json` on 8 ranges inside the 5 to 300 commit window (vite, babel, fastapi, nlohmann/json, renovate, pandoc and one unbounded pytest range): per-commit git calls (several per commit, plus `names gone`) do not scale. Needs batching or a work cap with a stated limit. 30 further cases took over 10 s (machine was shared, so load may explain part).

## Mapping differences, not necessarily bugs (decide)
- `scope` whitespace-only vs `git diff -w`: 9 of 84 (we use `-b --ignore-blank-lines`; stricter by design).
- stub markers vs `grep TODO|FIXME` on added lines: 9 of 84 (we count comment-syntax markers only).
- type-vs-files flag vs a simple re-derivation: 2 of 75 (config files).
- `check --diff` secrets vs gitleaks: 1 of 84 (gitleaks scans history, we scan the net diff).
- patch-id duplicates: 5 of 75, reference-side (rename detection); the one case checked matched `git show --no-renames`.

## Agreement
Commit count, revert links, cancelled set, duplicate literal text and summary cancelled matched the references on all 75 comparable ranges; zero stderr noise on success; no non-ASCII or large-file crashes.

Not covered: `facts`, `notes`, `claims`; the new `twins` and `names gone` features were not in the run.
