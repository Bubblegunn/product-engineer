Fix broken check-in reminders for guests with unresolved time zones

reminderHour() looked the guest's zone up in a hardcoded two-entry table
and returned `9 - undefined` on a miss, so the 4 guests with a null
timezone (ids 3, 6, 9, 12) were scheduled at hour NaN.

Read offsets from the IANA database via Intl instead. This fixes the miss
and also makes offsets DST-correct: the table had Europe/London pinned at
+1, which is wrong for the winter half of the year.

Guests whose zone cannot be resolved are now skipped by schedule() rather
than sent at a guessed hour, and are listed by the new unresolved() export
so the data can be corrected. Reminder times also carry minutes, for zones
at a half-hour offset, and wrap correctly when 09:00 local falls on the
adjacent UTC day.
