refactor: split pricing into a rule list and a calculator

`applies` condition and multiplier. src/pricing.js applies whatever is in the
list, in order, and rounds to cents; it knows nothing about individual rules.

dates in 2026 x 4 loyalty values x 4 lengths of stay) against a copy of the
pre-refactor function and asserts identical output.
