Retry the accounting webhook on transient failures

Payouts were going unrecorded in accounting whenever a single POST
failed. Retry up to 4 times with exponential backoff + jitter on
network errors, 429s, and 5xx. 4xx responses other than 429 fail fast
since a retry cannot help.

Two related behavior changes:
- Send an idempotency-key header (payout.id) so accounting can collapse
  duplicates when a retry follows a request that actually landed.
- Throw once attempts are exhausted instead of only logging, so callers
  can see the failure rather than silently dropping the payout.
