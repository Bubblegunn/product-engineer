The operative text starts at clause 1 below, and it governs. If this summary and a clause differ, the clause applies.

- Pay: You must pay EUR 500 within 30 days of the Effective Date. The fee is non-refundable.
- Transfer: You must not assign or sublicense your rights without the Licensor's prior written consent.
- End of the licence: If you do not fix a material breach within 15 days of written notice, the Licensor may end the licence immediately.
- Confidentiality: Even after the licence ends, you must keep the Licensor's customers' booking data confidential for 12 months.


WHO IS WHO

In this document, the Licensee is the party who holds the licence (you). The Licensor is the party who grants it. The Fee is the EUR 500 payment in clause 1.


THE CLAUSES

PAYMENT

1. Payment of the Fee
   1.1 The Licensee must pay the Licensor EUR 500 (the "Fee") within 30 days of the Effective Date.
   1.2 The Fee is non-refundable.


TRANSFER OF RIGHTS

2. Transfer of rights needs consent
   2.1 The Licensee must not assign any of its rights under this Licence (give them to someone else) without the Licensor's prior written consent.
   2.2 The Licensee must not sublicense any of its rights under this Licence (let someone else use them) without the Licensor's prior written consent.


TERMINATION

3. Ending the Licence for an unfixed breach
   3.1 Trigger: The Licensee commits a material breach of this Licence, and the Licensor gives the Licensee written notice of it.
   3.2 Obligation: The Licensee must remedy (fix) the breach within 15 days of that notice.
   3.3 Consequence: If the Licensee does not remedy the breach within those 15 days, the Licensor may terminate (end) this Licence immediately.


CONFIDENTIALITY

4. Confidentiality of booking data after the Licence ends
   4.1 The Licensee must keep the Licensor's customers' booking data confidential for 12 months after this Licence ends.
   4.2 This duty applies even though the Licence has ended.
