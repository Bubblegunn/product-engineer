Changes from 14 to 20 October 2026 (commit e7293a8 to 921093e)

## Search
You can now search bookings by guest name, so finding a guest's booking no longer means scrolling the whole list.
You can now filter bookings by date, so you can look at just the days you care about.
An empty search no longer crashes the page, so pressing search with nothing typed is safe.

## Billing
You can now download an invoice as a PDF, so sending one to a customer no longer needs a screenshot or a copy-paste.
Tax on invoices is now rounded to the cent, so amounts no longer carry fractions of a cent.

## Account and sign-in
You no longer get signed out while a form is open, so a long form you are filling in is not lost to an expired session.
