Changes from 14 to 20 October 2026 (commit e7293a8 to 921093e)

Search
You can now search bookings by guest name, so finding a guest's booking no longer means scrolling through the list.
You can now narrow bookings down to a date, so you can see only what falls on the day you care about.
An empty search no longer crashes the page; you now see the normal list instead.

Billing
You can now download an invoice as a PDF, so sending one to a customer no longer needs a screenshot or a copy-paste.
Tax on an invoice is now rounded to the nearest cent, so amounts no longer show fractions of a cent.

Sign-in
You no longer get signed out while you are in the middle of filling in a form, because the session now stays active for 30 minutes from your last activity.
