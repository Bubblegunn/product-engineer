Changes for 14 to 20 October 2026 (commit e7293a8 to 921093e)

**Search**
- You can now search bookings by guest name, so finding a booking no longer means scrolling through the whole list.
- You can now filter bookings by date, so you can look at just the days you care about.
- An empty search no longer crashes the page; it now stays usable.

**Billing**
- You can now download an invoice as a PDF, so sending one to a customer no longer means recreating it by hand.
- Tax on an invoice is now rounded to the nearest cent, so amounts no longer show fractions of a cent.

**Accounts and sign-in**
- You are no longer signed out while you are in the middle of filling in a form, because having a form open now keeps your session active.
