`docs/late-fees.md` is rewritten in simple language and committed as f9ba414. It is now short sentences under headings: how long you can keep an item, grace period, fee amounts, when you cannot borrow, and special rules.

I compared the new text with the old one rule by rule. All of these are unchanged:
- **Loan lengths:** 21 days, and 7 days for DVDs.
- **Renewals:** two renewals, not allowed if another patron has placed a hold.
- **Grace period:** 3 days, and DVDs get none.
- **Fees:** $0.25 per day for books and $1.00 per day for DVDs, capped at $10.00 per item.
- **Suspension:** borrowing stops at $5.00 or more owed and resumes below $5.00.
- **Under 12:** no late fees.
- **Lost items:** billed as lost 60 days after the due date.

I added no new rules or amounts.

I did not run any tests, because this is a docs-only change. I also did not have anyone else check that the wording is easy for a 10-year-old to follow.
