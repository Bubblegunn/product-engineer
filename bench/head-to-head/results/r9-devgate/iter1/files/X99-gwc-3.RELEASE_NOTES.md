# Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0.

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See your booked nights as a share of available nights, so you can tell at a glance how full your hotel is.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now go out at 9am your local time.** Previously they were sent at 9am UTC, which could be the middle of the night or the wrong time of day depending on where your hotel is.

## Improved

- **Faster CSV exports.** Exporting the same set of bookings again now returns much faster.
