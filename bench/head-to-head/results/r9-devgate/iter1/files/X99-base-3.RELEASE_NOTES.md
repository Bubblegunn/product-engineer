# Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0.

## What's new

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is at a glance. The report shows booked nights as a percentage of the nights you have available. A period with no available nights shows 0%.

## Improvements

- **Faster exports.** Exporting your bookings to CSV is faster when you export the same bookings again, for example after re-running an export without any changes.

## Fixes

- **Reminders now go out at 9am your local time.** Guest check-in reminders were being sent at 9am UTC, which could land at an awkward hour depending on where your hotel is. They are now sent at 9am in your hotel's local time on the day before check-in.
