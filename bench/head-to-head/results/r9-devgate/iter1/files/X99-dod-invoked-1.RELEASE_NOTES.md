# StayHub Release Notes

Everything new since v1.4.0.

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is at a glance. The report shows booked nights as a percentage of the nights you had available. A month with no bookings shows 0%.

## Improved

- **Reminders arrive at 9:00 am your time.** Guest reminders used to go out at 9:00 am UTC, which could be the middle of the night or the wrong part of the day depending on where your hotel is. They are now sent at 9:00 am local time on the day before check-in.
- **Faster CSV exports.** Exporting your bookings is quicker, and exporting the same list again is faster still.

## Fixed

- **Guest names with extra spaces.** Names entered with stray spaces, such as leading, trailing or doubled spaces, now display cleanly (for example, "Ada   Lovelace " appears as "Ada Lovelace").
