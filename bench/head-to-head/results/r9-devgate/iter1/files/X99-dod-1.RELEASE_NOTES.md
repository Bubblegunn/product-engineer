# StayHub — What's New Since v1.4.0

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is: the report shows booked nights as a percentage of the nights you had available, rounded to a whole number. A period with no available nights shows 0%. Months with no bookings are handled correctly.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now go out at 9am your local time.** Previously they were sent at 9am UTC, which could land at an inconvenient hour depending on where your hotel is.

## Improved

- **Faster repeat exports.** If you export the same set of bookings to CSV more than once, the repeat exports are now much faster. The first export of any new or changed set of bookings takes the same time as before.
