# Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0.

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See what share of your available room-nights are booked, shown as a whole percentage. If you have no available nights in a period, the report shows 0%.

## Improved

- **Faster exports.** Exporting your bookings to CSV is faster, particularly when you export the same set of bookings again.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now go out at 9am your local time.** Guest reminders were being sent at 9am UTC, which could arrive at an odd hour depending on where your hotel is. They are now sent at 9am on the day before check-in, in your hotel's local time.
