# StayHub 1.5.0 Release Notes

What's new since 1.4.0.

## Fixes

- **No more reminders for cancelled bookings.** Guests who cancel will no longer receive a check-in reminder email.

## Faster and smoother

- **Bookings list now loads in pages.** The list shows 50 bookings at a time, so it stays quick even when you have a lot of reservations.
- **Faster CSV export.** Exporting your bookings to CSV is now more efficient, which helps most with large exports.

## Documentation

- The README now explains that reminders are sent by email the day before check-in.

## Good to know

- Group bookings were tried during this cycle but pulled back out before release. They are **not** part of 1.5.0.
