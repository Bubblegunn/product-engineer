# StayHub – What's New Since v1.4.0

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is: booked nights as a percentage of the nights you had available, rounded to a whole number. A period with no available nights shows 0%.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now arrive at 9am your local time.** Guest reminders were previously sent at 9am UTC, which could land at an awkward hour depending on where your hotel is.

## Improved

- **Faster CSV exports.** Exporting the same set of bookings again is now much quicker.
