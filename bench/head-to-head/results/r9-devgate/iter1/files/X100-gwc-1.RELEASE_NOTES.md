# Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0, up to and including v1.6.0.

## New

- **JSON export.** Besides CSV, you can now export your bookings as JSON. Each entry includes the booking ID, guest name and check-in date. This is handy if you want to use your booking data in other tools.

## Fixed

- **Stay totals are now rounded to the cent.** Totals for a stay (nights × nightly rate) are rounded to 2 decimals, so you no longer risk seeing totals with stray extra digits, such as 0.30000000000000004 instead of 0.30.

## Under the hood

- Updated the CSV library we use for exports, and made small internal improvements.
