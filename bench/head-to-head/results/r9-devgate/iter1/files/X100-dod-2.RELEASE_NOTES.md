# Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0, up to and including v1.6.0.

## What's new

- **Export bookings as JSON.** In addition to CSV, you can now export your bookings as JSON (booking ID, guest name, and check-in date). This makes it easier to move your booking data into other tools.

## Fixes

- **Accurate stay totals.** Totals for a stay (number of nights × nightly rate) are now rounded to two decimal places, so you no longer see totals with stray fractions of a cent.

## Under the hood

- Updated the CSV library we use for exports to a newer version.
