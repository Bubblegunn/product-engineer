# Release Notes — Changes since v1.4.0

## What's new

- **Occupancy report.** You can now see your occupancy rate: the share of your available room-nights that were booked, shown as a whole percentage (for example, 15 of 30 nights booked is 50%). A period with no available nights shows 0% instead of an error.

## Improvements

- **Faster booking exports.** Exporting the same booking list again without changes is now much quicker, because the result is reused instead of rebuilt.

## Fixes

- **Guest reminders now arrive at 9:00 AM in your hotel's local time.** Previously reminders went out at 9:00 AM UTC, which could land in the middle of the night or at an awkward hour depending on where your hotel is. They still go out the day before check-in.
