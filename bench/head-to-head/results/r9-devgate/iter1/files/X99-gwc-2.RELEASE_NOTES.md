# What's New Since v1.4.0

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is: booked nights as a percentage of available nights, rounded to a whole number.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now arrive at 9am in your hotel's local time.** Guest reminders for the day before check-in used to go out at 9am UTC, which could be the wrong hour for hotels outside UTC. For example, a hotel at UTC+3 now sends at 9am local time instead of noon.

## Improved

- **Faster exports.** Exporting the same set of bookings again is now much quicker.
