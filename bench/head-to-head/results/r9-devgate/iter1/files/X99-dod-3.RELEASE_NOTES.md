# StayHub Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0.

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is: booked nights as a share of available nights, shown as a whole percentage. A period with no available nights shows 0%.

## Improved

- **Faster CSV exports.** Exporting the same set of bookings again is now much quicker.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now go out at 9am your local time.** Guest reminders were being sent at 9am UTC, which could be the wrong time of day for your hotel. They are now sent at 9am on the day before check-in, in your hotel's time zone.
