# StayHub Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0.

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is: booked nights as a share of available nights, shown as a whole percentage. If you have no available nights in the period, the report shows 0%.

## Improved

- **Faster CSV exports.** Exporting your bookings is now up to 3x faster, especially when you export the same set of bookings more than once.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now arrive at 9am your local time.** Guest reminders for the day before check-in used to go out at 9am UTC, which could be the wrong time of day depending on where your hotel is. They now go out at 9am in your hotel's time zone.
