Changes since v1.4.0 (commit 23e32d1 to e861353)

You can now export your bookings as a JSON file (a plain-text format that other software, such as spreadsheets and accounting tools, can read), so you can move your bookings into other tools without retyping them.
Stay totals are now rounded to the cent (2 decimal places), so a quote no longer shows a long trailing fraction when the nightly rate has cents.
