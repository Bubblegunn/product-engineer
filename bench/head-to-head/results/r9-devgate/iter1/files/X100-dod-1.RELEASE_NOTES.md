# Release Notes

## Changes since v1.4.0 (now version 1.6.0)

### New
- **JSON export of bookings.** Besides CSV, you can now export your bookings as JSON. Each entry includes the booking ID, guest name and check-in date, which makes it easier to use your data in other tools.

### Fixed
- **Stay totals are now rounded to the cent.** Totals for a stay (number of nights × nightly rate) are rounded to 2 decimals, so quotes no longer show stray fractions of a cent.

### Behind the scenes
- Updated the library behind CSV export and made small internal tidy-ups. You don't need to do anything.
