# Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0, up to version 1.6.0.

## New

- **JSON export of bookings.** In addition to CSV, you can now export your bookings as JSON. Each entry includes the booking ID, guest name, and check-in date. This makes it easier to use your booking data with other tools.

## Fixed

- **Stay totals are now rounded to two decimals.** Totals for a stay (nights × nightly rate) could previously show tiny fractional amounts for some rates, such as 0.30000000000000004 instead of 0.30. Totals now always show as a proper amount with at most two decimal places.

## Under the hood

- Updated the CSV library we use for exports, with no change to how exports look.
