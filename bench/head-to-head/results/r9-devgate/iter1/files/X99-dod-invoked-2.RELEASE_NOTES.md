# StayHub — What's New Since 1.4.0

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See how full your hotel is: booked nights as a share of available nights, shown as a whole percentage. A month with no bookings shows 0%.

## Improved

- **Faster CSV exports.** Exporting the same set of bookings again is now quicker, because the result is reused.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now go out at 9:00 AM in your hotel's local time.** Before, they were sent at 9:00 AM UTC, which could be the middle of the night or the wrong part of the day for hotels far from UTC. Guests still get the reminder the day before check-in.
