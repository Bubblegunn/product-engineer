# StayHub Release Notes: changes since v1.4.0

## What's new

### Occupancy report
You can now see how full your hotel is. The occupancy report shows your booked nights as a percentage of the nights you had available. The result is a whole number, so 72.4% shows as 72%. A month with no available nights shows 0%.

## Improvements

### Faster CSV exports
Exporting your bookings to CSV is now up to 3x faster. You will notice it most with large booking lists.

## Fixes

### Reminders now arrive at 9am your time
Guest reminders are sent at 9:00 AM on the day before check-in, in your hotel's local time. Before, they were sent at 9:00 AM UTC. Depending on where your hotel is, that could mean a reminder arriving in the middle of the night or at an awkward hour.

### Guest names with unusual spacing
Names with extra spaces, such as leading or trailing spaces or several spaces between words, now display cleanly.
