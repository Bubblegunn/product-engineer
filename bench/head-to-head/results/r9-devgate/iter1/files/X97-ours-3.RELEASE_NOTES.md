Changes since v1.4.0 (commit a5b89dd to dc71afd)

You can now search your bookings by guest name, so finding one reservation no longer means scrolling the whole list. Capital letters don't matter: "smith" finds "Smith".
Guest names that contain a comma, such as "Smith, Anna", now stay in one column when you export your bookings to a spreadsheet file, instead of spilling into the next column.
Exported booking files can no longer run instructions hidden in a guest name when you open them in a spreadsheet program.
The bookings list now shows 25 bookings at a time instead of 50.
