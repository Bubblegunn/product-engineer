# Release Notes

Changes since v1.4.0, up to and including v1.6.0.

## What's new

- **Export your bookings as JSON.** In addition to CSV, you can now export your bookings as JSON. Each entry includes the booking ID, guest name and check-in date, so the file is easy to use with other tools and spreadsheets.

## Fixes

- **Stay totals are now rounded to the cent.** The total for a stay (number of nights times your nightly rate) is now rounded to 2 decimal places. This removes stray fractions of a cent that could appear in quotes when your rate wasn't a round number.

## Behind the scenes

- We updated a supporting library used for CSV exports to its latest version.
