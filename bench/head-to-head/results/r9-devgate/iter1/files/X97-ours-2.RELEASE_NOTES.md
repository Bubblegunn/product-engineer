Changes since v1.4.0 (commit 1ee2019 to dc71afd)

You can now search your bookings by guest name, so finding a reservation no longer means scrolling the whole list. Capital and small letters are treated the same.
Exported booking spreadsheets can no longer run commands when you or a colleague open them, because guest names that start with a symbol spreadsheet programs treat as a formula are now saved as plain text.
Guest names that contain a comma now stay in a single column in exported spreadsheets, instead of splitting across two and pushing the check-in date out of place.
