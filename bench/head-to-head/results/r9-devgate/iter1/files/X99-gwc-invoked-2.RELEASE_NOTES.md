# Release Notes: since v1.4.0

## New

- **Occupancy report.** See what share of your available room-nights are booked, shown as a whole percentage. A month with no available nights shows 0% instead of an error.

## Improved

- **Faster exports.** Exporting your bookings to CSV is up to 3x faster, especially when you export the same data more than once.

## Fixed

- **Reminders now arrive at 9am your local time.** They were previously sent at 9am UTC, which could be the wrong time of day depending on where your hotel is.
