Changes since v1.4.0

You can now search your bookings by guest name, so finding a reservation no longer means scrolling through the whole list. Capital letters don't matter: "smith" finds "Smith".
Your bookings list now shows 25 bookings per page instead of 50, so each page is shorter to scroll through.
Guest names that contain a comma (for example "Smith, John") now stay in one column when you export bookings to a spreadsheet, instead of splitting across two.
Exported booking files can no longer run hidden instructions when you open them in a spreadsheet program, which protects your computer and your data.
