Changes since v1.4.0

You can now see an occupancy report, the share of your available room-nights that are booked, so you can tell at a glance how full your hotel was. A month with no available nights shows zero.
Arrival reminders can now go out at 9:00 in your hotel's own time on the day before check-in, so guests are no longer reminded at 9:00 in universal time (UTC, the world reference clock), which could be the middle of the night for you. Reminders still use UTC until your hotel's time difference from UTC is set.

Not in this release:
- Faster exports: exporting the same list of bookings again now reuses the earlier result, but no timing was measured, so no speed claim is made.
