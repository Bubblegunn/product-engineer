Changes since v1.4.0

You can now search your bookings by guest name, whatever capital letters you type, so finding one reservation no longer means scrolling the whole list.
Your bookings list now shows 25 bookings at a time instead of 50, so each page is shorter.
Guest names that contain a comma now stay in one column when you export bookings to a spreadsheet, so the names no longer split across columns.
Exported booking files can no longer run hidden spreadsheet formulas when you open them, which protects your computer and your data.
