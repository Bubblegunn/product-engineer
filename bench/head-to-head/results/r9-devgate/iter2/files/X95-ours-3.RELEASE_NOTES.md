Changes since v1.4.0

You can now browse your bookings list 50 bookings at a time, so a long list no longer has to load all at once (measured on a list of 10,000 bookings: 412 ms before, 38 ms after, median of 5 runs on a laptop; your device and list size will differ).
Guests with a cancelled booking no longer get a reminder; only bookings that are still on are reminded.

Not in this release:
- Group bookings: not ready yet, so they are not available.
