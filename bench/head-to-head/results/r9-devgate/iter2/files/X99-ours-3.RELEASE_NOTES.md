Changes since v1.4.0

You can now see occupancy, the share of your available room-nights that are booked, so you can tell at a glance how full a month was. A month with no available nights shows zero.
Reminders to guests can now go out at 9am in your hotel's own time zone instead of 9am UTC (the world time standard), so guests in your area no longer get them at an odd hour.
Exporting the same set of bookings again reuses the earlier result instead of rebuilding it.

Not in this release:
- Time zone setting for reminders: the reminder timing supports a local time zone, but nothing in this release sets your hotel's offset yet, so reminders still go out at 9am UTC until it is connected.
